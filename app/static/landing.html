The 6 Statutory Pillars of Digital Privacy

01 / VENDOR SHARING

Third-Party Data Sharing

Evaluates whether personal data is sold, monetized with ad brokers, or shared with commercial entities without explicit consent.

02 / RETENTION LIMITS

Data Retention Caps

Inspects whether the platform specifies exact deletion windows or retains personal telemetry indefinitely without user controls.

03 / TRACKER DENSITY

Cookies & Ad Beacons

Detects cross-site trackers, Meta Pixels, fingerprinting scripts, and non-essential third-party advertising cookies.

04 / STATUTORY RIGHTS

User Deletion & Erasure

Checks support for DPDP Section 12 consent withdrawal, GDPR Article 17 Right to Erasure, and self-service account deletion.

05 / SECURITY RECORD

Breach History & Safeguards

Cross-references verified OSINT public database leaks, unauthorized credential scraping, and past regulatory enforcement actions.

06 / PLAIN LANGUAGE

Readability & Clarity

Analyzes the linguistic readability score to ensure notices are transparent and understandable rather than hidden in legal jargon.
